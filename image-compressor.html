Image Compressor

Compress and resize images privately in your browser. Nothing is uploaded.

How image compression works here

Each image is drawn onto an HTML5 canvas, optionally resized, and re-encoded at the quality you choose. JPG and WebP use lossy compression, which can shrink photos by 60–90% with little visible change. PNG ignores the quality slider, so use resizing to make it smaller.

Frequently asked questions

Are my images uploaded anywhere?

No. Compression happens entirely on your device, which makes it safe for private photos and documents.

What quality setting should I use?

70–80% is the sweet spot for website photos. Go lower for thumbnails and higher for photos you may print.

Is camera metadata (EXIF) kept?

No. Re-encoding through canvas removes EXIF data such as GPS location, which is good for privacy.
