How to make a QR code

Enter a URL or text, choose the size and colors, then download the PNG. Codes are static and generated in your browser, so they never expire and are not tracked. Keep strong contrast between foreground and background so phones can scan it.

Frequently asked questions

Do these QR codes expire?

No. The data is stored directly in the QR pattern, so the code works forever without any server.

Which error correction level should I use?

Medium is fine for most uses. Choose High if the code will be printed small, may get damaged, or you plan to place a logo over it.
