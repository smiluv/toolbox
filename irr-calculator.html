What is IRR?

The internal rate of return is the discount rate at which the net present value of all cash flows equals zero: Σ CFt / (1 + IRR)t = 0. This calculator finds it numerically using the bisection method.

Frequently asked questions

What is a good IRR?

An investment is usually attractive when its IRR is higher than your cost of capital or required return. Many businesses look for an IRR above 10–15%.

Why does it say no IRR was found?

IRR only exists when the cash flows contain at least one negative and one positive value. Unusual cash-flow patterns can also have more than one IRR; this tool returns one of them.

What is the difference between IRR and ROI?

ROI is a simple total percentage gain. IRR accounts for the timing of every cash flow, so it is the better measure for multi-year projects.
