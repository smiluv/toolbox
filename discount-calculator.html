    How discounts are calculated

    Sale price = original × (1 − discount ÷ 100). A second discount is applied to the already-reduced price, so 25% off plus an extra 10% off is a 32.5% total discount, not 35%. Tax is added after discounts.

    Frequently asked questions

    Are two discounts the same as adding them together?

    No. Stacked discounts multiply: (1 − 0.25) × (1 − 0.10) = 0.675, which is 32.5% off.

    How do I calculate 30% off quickly?

    Multiply the price by 0.7. For example, 30% off 50 is 50 × 0.7 = 35.
