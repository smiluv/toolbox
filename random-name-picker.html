Random Name Picker Wheel

Enter one name per line, then spin the wheel to pick a random winner.

Is the wheel really random?

Yes. The final stopping angle is chosen with the browser's cryptographically secure random number generator, so every name has an equal chance. The winner is the segment under the pointer at the top.

Frequently asked questions

Can I use this for a giveaway or raffle?

Yes. Paste the entrants' names, spin, and screen-record the result for transparency. Tick "Remove winner" to draw several winners in a row.

Is there a limit on the number of names?

No hard limit, but the wheel stays readable up to about 100 names.
