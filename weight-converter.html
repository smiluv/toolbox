Frequently asked questions

How many pounds are in a kilogram?

One kilogram is about 2.20462 pounds.

What is the difference between a US ton and a metric ton?

A US (short) ton is 2,000 lb or 907.2 kg, while a metric ton (tonne) is 1,000 kg or about 2,204.6 lb.
