Temperature formulas

°F = °C × 9/5 + 32 · °C = (°F − 32) × 5/9 · K = °C + 273.15 · °R = °F + 459.67. Water freezes at 0 °C (32 °F) and boils at 100 °C (212 °F) at sea level.

Frequently asked questions

What is normal body temperature in Fahrenheit?

About 98.6 °F, which is 37 °C.

At what temperature are Celsius and Fahrenheit equal?

At −40 degrees: −40 °C is exactly −40 °F.
