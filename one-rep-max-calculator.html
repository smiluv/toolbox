Frequently asked questions

Is it safe to test a true one rep max?

Testing a true max carries injury risk, especially for beginners. Estimating it from a 3–8 rep set is safer and accurate enough for programming.

What percentage of 1RM should I train at?

Roughly 85–100% for maximal strength, 65–85% for muscle growth and below 65% for endurance and technique work.
