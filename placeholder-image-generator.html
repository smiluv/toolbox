How it works

The URL points to the free placehold.co image service, which draws the image on request. Paste it into any img tag, CSS background or design tool while the real images are not ready yet.

Frequently asked questions

Can I use these placeholder images in production?

They are meant for development and mockups. Replace them with real, optimized images before launch.

What size should a hero image be?

Common sizes are 1920×1080 for full-width hero banners, 1200×630 for social share images and 800×600 for blog thumbnails.
