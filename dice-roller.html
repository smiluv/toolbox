    How the dice roller works

    Each die is rolled with a cryptographically secure, unbiased random number generator, so every face has exactly the same chance. Results are summed and your modifier is added, just like "2d6+3" notation in tabletop games.

    Frequently asked questions

    What does 2d6 mean?

    It means roll two six-sided dice and add them together. "1d20+5" means roll one twenty-sided die and add 5.

    Are online dice fair?

    These are. Secure randomness without modulo bias makes them at least as fair as physical dice.
