White vs pink vs brown noise

White noise has equal energy at every frequency and sounds like TV static. Pink noise drops 3 dB per octave and sounds like steady rain, which many people find better for sleep. Brown noise is deeper still, like a waterfall or strong wind. All sounds are generated live with the Web Audio API, so there is nothing to download.

Frequently asked questions

Is white noise good for sleep?

Steady noise masks sudden sounds such as traffic or voices, which helps many people fall and stay asleep. Keep the volume low, around the level of a quiet shower.

What is a tone generator used for?

Testing speakers and headphones, tuning instruments (A4 = 440 Hz), checking which frequencies you can hear, and science experiments with sound.

Will it keep playing in the background?

Yes, as long as the tab stays open. On some phones the sound stops when the screen locks.
