Convert liquid and dry volume units for cooking, travel and science.

Common volume conversions

1 US gallon = 3.785 L · 1 imperial gallon = 4.546 L · 1 US cup = 236.6 ml · 1 US fluid ounce = 29.57 ml · 1 tablespoon = 3 teaspoons = 14.79 ml. US and imperial units have different sizes, so pick the right one.

Frequently asked questions

How many milliliters are in a cup?

A US customary cup is about 236.6 ml. Many recipes round this to 240 ml, and a metric cup is 250 ml.

Is a UK gallon bigger than a US gallon?

Yes. An imperial (UK) gallon is about 4.546 liters, roughly 20% more than a US gallon of 3.785 liters.
