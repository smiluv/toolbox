About these health and time tools

The health calculators use standard formulas such as BMI = weight ÷ height², the Mifflin-St Jeor equation for calories and Naegele's rule for due dates. The timers keep running accurately in the background and can sound an alert when time is up.

Frequently asked questions

Are these health calculators medical advice?

No. They give general estimates. Talk to a doctor or dietitian about your own health, diet or pregnancy.

Does the timer keep working if I switch tabs?

Yes. The timers use the real clock, so they stay accurate even when the tab is in the background.

Is my health data saved?

No. Your weight, height and dates are only used in your browser and are not stored or sent anywhere.
