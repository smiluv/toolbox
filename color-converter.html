Color formats explained

HEX and RGB describe red, green and blue channels from 0 to 255. HSL describes hue (0–360°), saturation and lightness, which is easier for creating shades. CMYK is used for print; values here are a simple conversion without a color profile.

Frequently asked questions

How do I convert HEX to RGB?

Split the 6-digit hex code into three pairs and convert each from base 16 to decimal: #2563EB becomes rgb(37, 99, 235).

Does it support transparency?

Yes. Enter an rgba(), hsla() or 8-digit hex color and the alpha channel is kept in every format except CMYK.
