URL Encoder / Decoder

Percent-encode text for use in URLs, or decode an encoded URL back to plain text.

Component vs full URL encoding

"Encode component" escapes every reserved character including / ? & = and is the right choice for a single query parameter value. "Encode full URL" keeps the URL structure intact and only escapes spaces and unsafe characters.

Frequently asked questions

What does %20 mean in a URL?

%20 is the percent-encoded form of a space character. Every unsafe character is written as % followed by its hexadecimal byte value.

Why do I see + instead of spaces?

HTML form submissions encode spaces as +. The decoder converts + back to spaces automatically.
