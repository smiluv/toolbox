Frequently asked questions

What BPM should I practice at?

Start slow enough to play a passage perfectly, then raise the tempo by 4–5 BPM at a time. Common tempos: ballads 60–80, pop 100–130, fast rock 140+.

What do the tempo names mean?

Largo is very slow (40–60), Adagio slow (66–76), Andante walking pace (76–108), Moderato moderate (108–120), Allegro fast (120–156) and Presto very fast (168–200).
