How the Pomodoro Technique works

Choose a task, focus for 25 minutes, then take a 5-minute break. After four focus sessions take a longer 15–30 minute break. The timer plays a sound when each session ends and keeps accurate time even when the tab is in the background.

Frequently asked questions

Why 25 minutes?

It is long enough to make real progress and short enough to stay fully focused. Many people adjust it to 45 or 50 minutes for deep work.

What should I do during a break?

Step away from the screen: stretch, walk, drink water or rest your eyes. Avoid email and social media.
