How to calculate a tip

Multiply the bill by the tip percentage: a 15% tip on 85 is 85 × 0.15 = 12.75. Add it to the bill and divide by the number of people to get each person's share.

Frequently asked questions

How much should I tip at a restaurant?

In the US 15–20% is standard for table service. Many other countries include a service charge or expect a smaller tip, so check local custom.

Should I tip on the amount before or after tax?

Tipping on the pre-tax amount is common and polite. Enter the pre-tax subtotal if you prefer that.
