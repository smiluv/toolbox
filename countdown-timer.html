How to share your countdown

Set the event name and date, then copy the share link. Anyone who opens it sees the same countdown in their own browser. Nothing is stored on a server; the event details live in the link itself.

Frequently asked questions

Which time zone does the countdown use?

The date and time you enter are in your local time zone. Visitors in other zones see a countdown to the same moment shown in their local time.

Will it alert me when the time is up?

Yes, if the page is open it plays a short sound and shows a "Time's up" message.
