How the payoff is calculated

The regular payment uses the standard amortization formula M = L·i / (1 − (1 + i)−N) with monthly rate i and N months. The calculator then simulates the loan month by month with and without your extra payment, applying every extra dollar directly to principal.

Frequently asked questions

Is it worth paying extra on a mortgage?

Extra payments reduce principal early, which lowers the interest charged in every later month. Even small extra amounts can save years of payments and a large amount of interest.

Does this include taxes and insurance?

No. The payment shown is principal and interest only. Property tax, insurance and PMI are added on top by most lenders.
