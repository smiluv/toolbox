How accurate is this stopwatch?

It uses the browser's high-resolution performance clock, so timing stays accurate even if the tab is in the background or the display refreshes slowly. Lap time is the time since the previous lap; total is the time since you started.

Frequently asked questions

Does the stopwatch keep running if I switch tabs?

Yes. Time is measured from the start moment rather than counted frame by frame, so switching tabs does not affect it.

What keyboard shortcuts are available?

Space starts and stops, L records a lap and R resets the stopwatch.
