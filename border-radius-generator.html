border-radius shorthand

  
border-radius: top-left top-right bottom-right bottom-left; Values in % are relative to the element's size, so 50% on all corners makes a circle or ellipse. Large unequal values create organic blob shapes.

  
Frequently asked questions

  
How do I make a circle with CSS?
Give a square element border-radius: 50%.

  
How do I make a pill-shaped button?
Use a very large px value such as border-radius: 999px; it is limited to half the element's height automatically.
