Which barcode type should I use?

Code 128 encodes any text and is ideal for shipping labels and inventory. EAN-13 and UPC-A are retail product codes (12–13 digits with a check digit). ITF-14 is used on cartons, and Code 39 is common in industry and ID badges.

Frequently asked questions

Can I use these barcodes on products I sell in stores?

Retail EAN/UPC numbers must be registered with GS1. This tool draws the barcode, but the number itself has to come from your GS1 membership.

Why is my value invalid?

Each format has rules: EAN-13 needs 12 or 13 digits, UPC-A needs 11 or 12 digits, and the last digit must be a correct check digit if you include it.
