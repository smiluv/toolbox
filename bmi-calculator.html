BMI formula and categories

BMI = weight (kg) ÷ height (m)². WHO adult categories: under 18.5 underweight, 18.5–24.9 healthy weight, 25–29.9 overweight, 30 and above obesity. BMI is a screening tool and does not measure body fat directly; athletes, older adults and some ethnic groups may need different cut-offs.

Frequently asked questions

What is a healthy BMI?

For most adults a BMI between 18.5 and 24.9 is considered a healthy weight.

Is BMI accurate for muscular people?

No. Muscle is heavier than fat, so very muscular people can have a high BMI while being lean. Waist size or body-fat measurements give a fuller picture.

Can children use this BMI calculator?

No. Children and teens need age- and sex-specific BMI percentiles. This calculator is for adults aged 18 and over.
