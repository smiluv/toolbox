Frequently asked questions

How do I calculate a percentage increase?

Subtract the old value from the new value, divide by the old value and multiply by 100. From 80 to 100 is a 25% increase.

Is a 50% drop followed by a 50% rise back to the start?

No. 100 down 50% is 50, and 50 up 50% is only 75. Percentages are always relative to the starting value.
