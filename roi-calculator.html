ROI formula

ROI = (Final value − Investment) / Investment × 100. The annualized ROI, also called CAGR, is (Final / Investment)1/years − 1, which lets you compare investments held for different lengths of time.

Frequently asked questions

What is a good ROI?

It depends on risk. Broad stock market indexes have historically returned around 7–10% per year on average, so many investors use that as a benchmark.

Why is annualized ROI lower than total ROI?

Total ROI covers the whole holding period. Annualized ROI spreads that growth across each year with compounding, so a 50% gain over 3 years is about 14.5% per year.
