What a GST tax invoice must show

Under GST rules a tax invoice shows the supplier's name, address and GSTIN; a serial invoice number and date; the customer's name, address and GSTIN (if registered); the place of supply; each item's description, HSN or SAC code, quantity, value, discount and taxable value; the GST rate and the CGST, SGST/UTGST or IGST amount; whether tax is payable on reverse charge; and a signature. This generator lays all of that out for you.

CGST + SGST or IGST? If your state and the place of supply are the same, tax is split equally into CGST and SGST (UTGST in Chandigarh, Ladakh, Lakshadweep, Andaman and Nicobar, and Dadra and Nagar Haveli and Daman and Diu). If they differ, the full rate is charged as IGST. The generator picks this automatically from the two states.

Bill of supply is used instead of a tax invoice by composition dealers and for exempt goods or services; it does not charge GST. A proforma invoice or quotation is an offer, not a tax invoice.

Frequently asked questions

How do I save the invoice as PDF?

Click "Print / Save as PDF" and choose "Save as PDF" as the printer. Only the invoice is printed, on A4.

Is my data uploaded anywhere?

No. Everything stays in your browser. Your business and bank details are remembered in this browser's local storage so you don't have to type them again; "Clear everything" deletes them.

How is the GSTIN checked?

A GSTIN has 15 characters: a 2-digit state code, the 10-character PAN, an entity number, the letter Z and a check character. The generator checks the format and the check character, and fills in your state from the code.

How is the amount written in words?

In the Indian numbering system with lakh and crore, for example ₹1,23,45,678 is "Rupees One Crore Twenty Three Lakh Forty Five Thousand Six Hundred Seventy Eight Only".

Which GST rate should I use?

Most goods and services are 5% or 18% since 22 September 2025, with 40% for luxury and sin goods, 3% for gold and silver and 0% for exempt items. Check your HSN or SAC code on the CBIC website, or work out the tax first with our GST calculator.
