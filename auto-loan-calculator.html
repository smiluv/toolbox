    How the car payment is calculated

    Sales tax is applied to the price minus your trade-in. The amount financed is price plus tax minus down payment and trade-in. The monthly payment uses the standard loan amortization formula.

    Frequently asked questions

    Is a longer car loan better?

    A longer term lowers the monthly payment but increases the total interest you pay. Compare 48, 60 and 72 months to see the trade-off.

    How much should I put down on a car?

    A common guideline is 10–20% of the price. A bigger down payment means a smaller loan, lower payments and less interest.
