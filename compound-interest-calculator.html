    How compound interest is calculated

    The classic formula is A = P(1 + r/n)nt, where P is the starting amount, r the annual rate, n the compounding periods per year and t the number of years. This calculator also adds your monthly contribution at the end of every month and shows a year-by-year table of contributions, interest and balance.

    Frequently asked questions

    What is compound interest?

    Compound interest is interest earned on your original money and on the interest already added to it. Over long periods it makes savings grow exponentially instead of in a straight line.

    Does daily compounding earn more than monthly?

    Yes, slightly. The more often interest compounds, the higher the effective annual yield, but the difference between daily and monthly compounding is small.

    What is the rule of 72?

    Divide 72 by the annual interest rate to estimate how many years it takes to double your money. At 7% that is roughly 10 years.
