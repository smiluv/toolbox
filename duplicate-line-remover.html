Duplicate Line Remover

Paste a list and get only unique lines back, in their original order.

How it works

Each line is compared with the lines before it. The first occurrence is kept and later copies are removed, so the order of your list is preserved.

Frequently asked questions

Can I remove duplicates without changing the order?

Yes, that is exactly what this tool does. Use the text alphabetizer afterwards if you also want the list sorted.

Is there a limit on how many lines I can paste?

No fixed limit. Tens of thousands of lines are processed instantly in your browser.
