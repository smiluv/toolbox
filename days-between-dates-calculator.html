How the days are counted

Dates are compared in UTC, so daylight saving changes never add or remove an hour. By default the start date is counted and the end date is not, the same way most contracts and deadlines work. Business days exclude Saturdays and Sundays but not public holidays.

Frequently asked questions

Should I include the end date?

Include it when both the first and last day count, for example the length of a hotel stay in days or a vacation from Monday to Friday inclusive.

Are public holidays excluded from business days?

No. Holidays differ by country, so subtract any public holidays in your period manually.
