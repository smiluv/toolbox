    How TDS is calculated

    TDS on salary (section 192) is your estimated income tax for the year, under the regime you chose with your employer, spread over the remaining months of the financial year (April to March) after subtracting TDS already deducted. It uses the same slabs, standard deduction, 87A rebate, surcharge and 4% cess as our income tax calculator.

    Other payments: TDS = amount × rate, once the payment crosses the threshold for that section. For cash withdrawals (194N) and purchase of goods (194Q) TDS applies only to the amount above the limit. Without a valid PAN, section 206AA applies the higher of twice the rate or 20% (5% for 194O and 194Q). Rates and thresholds follow the Finance Act 2025, and section numbers follow the Income-tax Act, 1961, which most people still use; check current rules on the Income Tax Department website before filing.

    Frequently asked questions

    How much TDS will be deducted from my salary?

    Your employer estimates your full-year tax under your chosen regime and deducts it in equal monthly instalments. Under the new regime, salary up to ₹12.75 lakh usually has no TDS because of the ₹75,000 standard deduction and the 87A rebate.

    What is the TDS rate on professional fees?

    Section 194J: 10% on professional fees and royalty, 2% on fees for technical services, once payments to the person cross ₹50,000 in the financial year.

    When is TDS deducted on rent?

    Under section 194I, tenants who must deduct TDS do so at 10% on land and building or 2% on plant and machinery when rent exceeds ₹50,000 a month. Individuals not under audit use section 194IB at 2% when monthly rent exceeds ₹50,000.

    What happens if the payee does not give a PAN?

    TDS is deducted at a higher rate: twice the normal rate or 20%, whichever is higher (5% for e-commerce and purchase-of-goods TDS).

    Can I get TDS back?

    Yes. TDS is an advance payment of tax. If it is more than your actual tax for the year, claim the difference as a refund when you file your income tax return.
