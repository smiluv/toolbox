    How the split works

    Shared items are divided equally between everyone. Each person's own items are added to their share, then tax and tip are applied to that subtotal, so people who ordered more also pay more tax and tip. For a simple even split, enter the whole bill as shared items and leave personal items at zero.

    Frequently asked questions

    What is the fairest way to split a bill?

    Splitting by what each person ordered, with shared dishes divided evenly and tax and tip in proportion, is generally considered the fairest method.
