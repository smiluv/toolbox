Salary to Hourly Wage Converter

Convert pay between hourly, daily, weekly, bi-weekly, monthly and yearly amounts.

How the conversion works

Your pay is first converted to a yearly amount using your hours per week, days per week and paid weeks per year, then divided back into every other pay period. Use fewer paid weeks if you take unpaid time off.

Frequently asked questions

How many working hours are in a year?

A full-time schedule of 40 hours a week for 52 weeks is 2,080 hours, which is why hourly ≈ yearly salary ÷ 2,080.

Is this before or after tax?

The results are gross pay before tax. Use the income tax estimator to approximate your take-home pay.
