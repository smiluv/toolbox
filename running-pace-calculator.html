How pace is calculated

Pace = time ÷ distance, time = pace × distance, and distance = time ÷ pace. The table shows how long common race distances take at this pace. Real race times also depend on terrain, weather and fatigue over longer distances.

Frequently asked questions

What pace do I need for a sub-4-hour marathon?

About 5:41 per km or 9:09 per mile, held for all 42.195 km.

What is a good 5K time for beginners?

Many beginners finish a 5K in 30–40 minutes, which is a pace of about 6–8 minutes per km.
