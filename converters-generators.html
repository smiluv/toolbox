About these converters and generators

The converters show every related unit at once, so you can read kilograms, pounds and ounces side by side. The time zone converter handles daylight saving automatically. Generators create QR codes and barcodes as downloadable images, and the random tools use a fair, unbiased random source.

Frequently asked questions

Do the QR codes expire?

No. The QR code contains your text or link directly, so it works forever and does not depend on this website.

Is the random name picker fair?

Yes. It uses the browser's cryptographic random generator, so every name has an equal chance.

How accurate are the unit conversions?

They use exact international definitions, for example 1 inch = 2.54 cm and 1 pound = 0.45359237 kg, shown to many significant figures.
