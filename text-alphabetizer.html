Text Alphabetizer & List Sorter

Put any list in alphabetical order or sort it the way you need.

Sorting options explained

A → Z uses language-aware alphabetical order. Natural sort treats numbers as numbers, so "item2" comes before "item10". Shuffle uses a cryptographically secure random generator.

Frequently asked questions

How do I alphabetize a comma-separated list?

Choose "Commas" as the separator. The result is returned as a comma-separated list in sorted order.
