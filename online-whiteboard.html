Online Whiteboard

Draw with your mouse, finger or stylus, then save your sketch as an image.

How to use the whiteboard

Pick a color and brush size and start drawing. Use the eraser to rub out parts, Undo (or Ctrl+Z) to step back, and Save PNG to download your drawing. Pressure-free drawing works with a mouse, touchscreen or pen tablet.

Frequently asked questions

Is my drawing saved online?

No. The drawing exists only in this browser tab. Click Save PNG before closing the page to keep it.

Can I use it on a phone or tablet?

Yes. Touch and stylus input are supported, and page scrolling is disabled while drawing on the board.
