How it is calculated

BMR uses the Mifflin-St Jeor equation: 10 × kg + 6.25 × cm − 5 × age + 5 for men and … − 161 for women. TDEE is BMR multiplied by your activity factor. A deficit of about 500 calories per day leads to roughly 0.5 kg (1 lb) of weight loss per week.

Frequently asked questions

What is the difference between BMR and TDEE?

BMR is the energy your body uses at complete rest. TDEE adds the calories burned by daily movement and exercise, so it is the number to eat to maintain your weight.

What is the minimum safe calorie intake?

As a general guide, avoid going below about 1,200 calories per day for women and 1,500 for men without medical supervision.
