Where do the rates come from?

Rates are loaded from the free Rates By Exchange Rate API and refresh once a day. They are mid-market reference rates; banks and card providers usually add a margin.

Frequently asked questions

Are these real-time exchange rates?

They are updated daily, which is accurate enough for travel budgets, invoices and price comparisons, but not for trading.

Why is my bank's rate different?

Banks and exchange offices add a spread or fee on top of the mid-market rate shown here.
