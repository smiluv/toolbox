About these finance calculators

Every calculator runs in your browser, so your salary, loan and business figures never leave your device. The India tools follow current rules: the new and old income tax regimes with the ₹75,000 standard deduction and 87A rebate, TDS rates and thresholds by section, and the GST 2.0 rates in force since 22 September 2025. Loan calculators use the same reducing-balance maths banks use, including interest rest periods and prepayments.

Start with the new tax regime calculator, check TDS on salary, work out 18% GST, or make a GST tax invoice.

Frequently asked questions

Are these tax calculators updated for FY 2026-27?

They use the Finance Act 2025 rules (FY 2025-26), which continue for FY 2026-27 unless a later Budget changes them. Always confirm with the Income Tax Department or a chartered accountant before filing.

Which calculator tells me whether the new or old tax regime is better?

The income tax calculator compares both regimes for your income and deductions and shows how much deduction you would need for the old regime to win.

Can I make a GST bill for my shop or business?

Yes. The GST invoice generator makes a printable tax invoice with GSTIN, HSN/SAC codes, CGST + SGST or IGST, amount in words and a UPI QR code, and saves your business details in your browser.

Is my financial data stored anywhere?

No. All calculations happen on your device. Only the currency converter downloads public exchange rates.
