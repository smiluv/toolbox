How counting works

Words are groups of characters separated by spaces. Reading time assumes an average silent reading speed of 238 words per minute and speaking time 130 words per minute. Top keywords ignore common short words.

Frequently asked questions

How many words are on a page?

About 500 words single-spaced or 250 words double-spaced with a 12-point font.

How long does it take to read 1,000 words?

Roughly 4 minutes at an average reading speed, and about 7–8 minutes to say out loud.
