Time Zone Converter

Pick a date, time and time zone to see the matching time around the world.

How it works

The converter uses the official IANA time zone database built into your browser, so daylight saving time changes are applied automatically for the selected date. Your list of time zones is remembered on this device.

Frequently asked questions

What is the difference between UTC and GMT?

For everyday use they are the same time. UTC is the precise atomic time standard; GMT is the time zone used by the UK in winter.

Does this handle daylight saving time?

Yes. Offsets are calculated for the exact date you choose, including DST start and end dates in every region.
