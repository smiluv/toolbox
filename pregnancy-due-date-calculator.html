How the due date is calculated

Pregnancy is counted from the first day of the last menstrual period (LMP). The due date is LMP + 280 days (Naegele's rule), adjusted for cycles longer or shorter than 28 days. From conception it is + 266 days; for IVF it is transfer date + 263 days (day 3) or + 261 days (day 5).

This is an estimate for information only. Only about 4% of babies arrive on their due date. Always follow the dates given by your doctor or midwife, especially after an ultrasound scan.

Frequently asked questions

How many weeks is a full-term pregnancy?

A full-term pregnancy is 39 to 40 weeks from the last menstrual period. Babies born from 37 weeks are considered early term.

When does each trimester start?

The first trimester is weeks 1–13, the second is weeks 14–27 and the third is from week 28 until birth.
