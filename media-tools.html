About these media tools

The image compressor resizes and re-encodes photos on your device, which is faster and more private than uploading them to a server. The sound tools use the Web Audio API for sample-accurate timing and clean tones.

Frequently asked questions

Are my images uploaded when I compress them?

No. Compression happens entirely in your browser and the result downloads straight to your device.

Will the metronome stay in time?

Yes. Beats are scheduled ahead on the audio clock rather than with page timers, so the tempo stays steady.

Can white noise help me sleep or focus?

Many people find steady noise masks distractions. Brown noise is deeper and pink noise is softer than white noise; try each at a low volume.
